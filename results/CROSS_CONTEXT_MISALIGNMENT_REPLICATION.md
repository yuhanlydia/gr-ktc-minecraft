# Cross-Context Consolidation Misalignment: Raw-Trajectory Replication

## Decision

The Grassmann-misalignment hypothesis did not replicate. Stop the Grassmann
Transport / SMC method branch under the preregistered rule.

## Independent replication design

- Eight mixed-outcome MineExplorer contexts: `0000`, `0076`, `0251`, `0281`,
  `0299`, `0391`, `0499`, `0549`.
- Eight newly sampled rollouts per context (64 total), seeds 500–507.
- Qwen3-VL-8B, BF16, Minecraft 1.19 + Voyager/Mineflayer, no API.
- Raw layer-24 K/V trajectories saved before merging.
- Each successful trajectory was phase-aligned to 32 positions and differenced
  against its context's failed-trajectory barycenter.
- Latent feature subspaces use rank-2 right singular vectors, fixed from the
  four-context pilot.
- All 28 context pairs received a newly fitted rank-8 shared reachability
  proxy; interference is mean individual rho minus pair-shared rho.
- Replication primary: Key-space Grassmann distance.
- Gate: positive Spearman, 100,000-permutation `p<=0.05`, and context-cluster
  bootstrap 95% lower confidence bound above zero.

## Result

| Geometry | Spearman | Permutation p | Context-bootstrap 95% CI |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Key (primary) | 0.159 | 0.417 | [-0.714, 0.833] |
| Value | 0.048 | 0.809 | [-0.638, 0.765] |
| Joint K+V | 0.141 | 0.475 | [-0.760, 0.814] |
| Mean(K,V) | 0.072 | 0.717 | [-0.735, 0.722] |

The primary fails every inferential criterion. The four-context Key result
(`rho=0.829`, exact `p=0.058`) was therefore a small-sample or merged-support
artifact, not sufficient evidence for Cross-Context Consolidation
Misalignment.

The eight-context consensus top-2 eigenvalue means are `0.479` for Key,
`0.404` for Value, and `0.465` jointly, but these spectra do not predict the
observed pairwise LoRA interference.

## Consequence

Do not implement Grassmann Transport, Consensus-Private/Spectral Memory
Consolidation, or Grassmann Mixtures from this evidence. The established
phenomenon remains that shared reachability drops with additional contexts,
but native-KV principal-angle misalignment is not its demonstrated cause.
